## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a **Product Designer with 18+ years of experience** working across design, technology and digital products.

My background combines **Product Design, UI/UX, Design Systems, prototyping and front-end**, allowing me to work closely with both product and engineering teams — from early concepts to polished experiences.

Today, I'm especially interested in the intersection of **Design + AI + Code**, exploring new ways of designing and building products faster without sacrificing craft.

I've worked with teams and products across **Brazil, Portugal, Germany, the Netherlands and Canada**.
